## Rules

- Preserve source attribution and NASA media-usage guidance.
- Do not use NASA names, insignia, seals, or identifiers to imply sponsorship, endorsement, government affiliation, certification, or approval of XUNIA products.
- Keep repository licenses and upstream-project attribution unchanged.
- Prefer lightweight preview assets in READMEs; do not embed multi-megabyte TIFF/model payloads as page headers.
- Project-specific scenes may use other assets from this repository when their provenance and usage conditions are documented.

## Source

Upstream resource metadata identifies `nasa/NASA-3D-Resources` as the source collection. See this repository's `README.md` and `meta.json` for provenance and usage information.
